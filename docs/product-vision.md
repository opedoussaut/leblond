# LEBLOND — A free, community-first climbing intelligence platform

LEBLOND remains free to use for anyone who registers. No premium features, subscription or paywall at this stage. Reassess commercialization only after the user community validates the product.

## Two complementary but independent journeys

### A. Climbing diary (no plan required)

Log in, select language, set separate boulder and route levels and goals, select gyms, record sessions and see real progress. Never force a coaching method or subscription. This is a complete standalone experience.

### B. Guided progression with Patrick (optional)

The same climber can elect to:
1. Establish a baseline from actual climbs plus an explicitly subjective, anchored six-skill evaluation.
2. Set one or two goals (bouldering, route, both), training time available each week and timeframe.
3. Compare methods, programs and tools by described emphasis, available evidence and personal fit. Radar emphasis is NOT proof of effectiveness.
4. Use a simulator with editable assumptions to explore scenarios, including a no-program option, with uncertainty rather than guaranteed grade forecasts.
5. Choose or edit a plan, or decline to use one.
6. Compare simulated vs actually recorded progress, adherence and skill check-ins. Patrick explains observed changes and suggests reasonable adjustments.

All logged sessions belong to one history, irrespective of whether they were created under a plan.

## Separation of evidence

- Measured data: climbing discipline, attempts, completions, sessions, exposure, grades, recorded frequency and trends.
- Self-reported data: six skill ratings, goals, fatigue, preferences, perceived confidence.
- Simulator output: scenario with inspectable assumptions and appropriate uncertainty. Not a validated causal training efficacy estimate or promise of a grade by a date.
- Current six-axis self-assessment and illustrative chart are already available but are NOT a calibrated outcome prediction.

## Core deliverables and status

1. Available: open free registration with Google/email, bilingual onboarding, separate bouldering/route reported levels and targets, facility directory, boulder logs and progress, training method and tool comparison, plans, skill self-assessments and check-ins.
2. Next: true route-session capture (lead/top rope, route grade, attempts, redpoint/onsight), independent boulder/route trends and working grades; separate but comparable timelines.
3. Next: merge logged climbs into assessments, calculate clearly evidenced capability estimates, distinguish subjective ratings from observed results, support both disciplines.
4. Next: simulator driven by current assessment, target, available weeks, training hours/sessions, method choices and equipment. Show explicit assumptions, scenarios and likely limiting factors; no unsupported grade forecasts.
5. Next: Patrick-assisted plan creation and iterative review of adherence, progress observed vs scenario; never a requirement to use a plan.

## Public/free and privacy

Open sign-up, no subscription and free features. Public educational content (catalogue and method references) is shareable. Personal logs, plans and assessments remain private by default under authentication and row-level security. Optional public sharing should require explicit consent.

## Product and safety constraints

- Route and boulder grades must never be auto-converted or conflated.
- Current actual working-grade analytics cover bouldering only. Route targets can be saved but do not imply an implemented route logging or progress engine.
- Never present speculative scenarios as measured performance or scientifically validated predictions.
- Mobile-first premium blue/steel-gray UI with equal entry paths for free tracking and optional coaching.
- Evaluate commercial options only if the freely accessible version succeeds with friends and early community users.
